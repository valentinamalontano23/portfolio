Privacy Policy

How your data is handled here.

This site is a portfolio. It has no accounts, no shop and no tracking. This page describes exactly what happens to personal data, and nothing more than what actually happens.

Last updated 20 October 2026 · Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Valentina D'Ambrosio, an independent creator based in Italy, reachable at va.lentina.ma.lontano23 [at] gmail.com. A postal address is given on request to that address — it is not published here, because nothing on this site requires it and publishing a private home address would be a worse outcome for everyone.

No Data Protection Officer has been appointed: the processing carried out here does not meet the criteria in Article 37 GDPR.

2. What this site does not do

Stated plainly, because it is unusual enough to be worth saying:

  • No cookies. The site sets none — not analytics, not advertising, not functional.
  • No analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no heatmap or session recording of any kind.
  • No forms. There is nothing to fill in. Contact happens through your own email client.
  • No profiling and no automated decision-making.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared for anyone else's marketing.
  • Fonts are served from this site, not from Google Fonts, so no request carries your IP address to a third party when the page loads.

3. Server logs

Like every site on the internet, the hosting provider records technical data when a page is requested: IP address, date and time, the page requested, browser and operating system, and the referring page.

  • Purpose: delivering the site, security, diagnosing faults and preventing abuse.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — keeping the service running and secure.
  • Processor: GitHub, Inc. — a Microsoft company, San Francisco, United States — which hosts this site on GitHub Pages and delivers it through the Fastly content delivery network, acting under Article 28 GDPR.
  • Retention: the provider keeps these logs for as long as it needs them to operate and protect the service. It does not publish a fixed period, and the logs are not accessible to the controller.
  • Transfer outside the EU: the provider is a US company, so log data may be processed in the United States. The transfer rests on the standard contractual clauses in the provider's data protection agreement and, where applicable, on its certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

4. If you get in touch

The contact buttons open your own email program. Nothing is submitted through this site. Once you write, the data in your message — name, email address, company, and whatever else you choose to include — is processed in order to read and answer it.

  • Purpose: replying to your enquiry and, where relevant, preparing or negotiating a collaboration.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; and legitimate interest in answering enquiries, Article 6(1)(f).
  • Retention: for as long as the exchange is live, and afterwards for as long as tax, accounting or limitation periods require. Correspondence that leads nowhere is deleted within 24 months.
  • Where the message is stored: the mailbox is a Gmail account, so Google stores the message on its servers and may process it outside the EU, under its own privacy policy and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Google is not acting as a processor on the controller's behalf for a consumer account.

Providing this data is not a legal obligation, but without an email address there is no way to answer you.

5. The videos

The videos in the project cards are files served from this site itself. There is no YouTube, Vimeo or other video platform involved — no embed, no player script, no third-party cookie, and no request leaving this domain when you press play. The only party that sees the request is the hosting provider named in section 3.

This is deliberate: an embedded player would hand your IP address and a set of cookies to a company you did not choose to visit. If an external embed is ever added, it will not load until you click to allow it, and this page will be updated before that happens.

6. Links to other sites

Links to Instagram or to collaborators' sites take you off this site. What happens there is governed by their privacy policies, not this one.

7. Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 22 GDPR you may ask to:

  • know what data is held and obtain a copy of it;
  • have it corrected if it is wrong or incomplete;
  • have it erased;
  • restrict how it is used;
  • receive it in a portable, machine-readable format;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was lawful before.

Write to va.lentina.ma.lontano23 [at] gmail.com. You will get an answer within one month. If you are not satisfied you can complain to the Italian supervisory authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority where you live.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, so traffic between your browser and the server is encrypted. Appropriate technical and organisational measures are in place, as required by Article 32 GDPR.

9. Changes

If what the site does changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated. Material changes will be made evident.
